## For future Claude
Ediciones Gamma is the publishing house of Grupo Bolívar / Banco Davivienda, founded 1979, based in Bogotá. Operates 6 business units: Diners, AXXIS, Libros, Experiencias, Visión Davivienda, and Educación Financiera. Critical 2026 context: Privilegios Davivienda closes June 2026, causing 70% revenue loss for Revistas. New strategic umbrella: "Mundo D".
