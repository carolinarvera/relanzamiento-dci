Positioning strategy · two paths

Re-creating belonging.

AON — Always On

An ongoing platform of curated benefits featuring the country's best dining offerings. Not simple coupons — selected experiences — the natural territory of Diners Magazine, driving the repositioning through perceived value.

Interesting Life

The most exclusive, inspirational side of the partnership. Private events, premium entertainment, and chef-driven gastronomy designed to uncover local hidden gems. Unmatched moments that redefine what it means to belong to a selective club.

The strategy communicates concrete, verifiable value: real discounts at curated merchants. Every piece of communication points to a benefit that can be activated that same month.

The plan

Aligning the moves.

The goal: connect Diners Club with Colombia's premium lifestyle.

  1. Always-on benefits: cardholder access to the Beneficios Suscriptores platform.
  2. Flagship annual benefit delivered in a special issue of the magazine.
  3. Exclusive experiences activated by paying with the Diners card (franchise budget generation).
  4. Section sponsorship: Diners Club as a brand-presence pillar across the magazine's full ecosystem (print, web, social, La Mesa de Diners, digital formats), building presence and positioning.
  5. "Diners Club Recomienda" special section with a seasonal calendar.
