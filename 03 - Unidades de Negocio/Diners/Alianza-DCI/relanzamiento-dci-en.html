Module 1 · Brand Relaunch

Making the connection.

The approach, the content hooks, and who we're talking to.

The Strategic Spender

High-net-worth. Expects the world to work around him, not the other way around.

  • Profile: 45+. Strong buying power and plenty of liquidity.
  • Where they are in life: Wants total comfort. Balancing a heavy workload with family time.
  • What they're into: Business-class tickets, high-end hotels, fine dining, international insurance, golf and private clubs, streaming.
  • The Diners challenge: They carry it for the status, but there's real risk they reach for Mastercard Black or Visa Infinite instead.
  • The opening: Priority access. Experiences they can't get anywhere else.
The Traditional Spender

The quiet workhorse. Uses the card out of habit and loyalty to the bank.

  • Profile: 51+. Long-tenured professionals.
  • Where they are in life: Family first. Trips are planned well ahead. They value their time and their peace of mind.
  • What they're into: Travel, standout stays, and good food.
  • The Diners challenge: Passive use, mostly autopay.
  • The opening: Anchor events backed by the franchise.
The Active Value Spender

Peak earning years. Highly active wherever experiences happen.

  • Profile: 35+. High and upper-middle income brackets.
  • Where they are in life: Ahead of the curve on food and culture. Values ideas, art, and design.
  • What they're into: High-end tech, digital platforms, the restaurants everyone's talking about.
  • The Diners challenge: Heavy competition for everyday spend and card swipes.
  • The opening: VIP experiences.
